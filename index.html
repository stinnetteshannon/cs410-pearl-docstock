DocStock

A reminder a day keeps Doc at bay.

DocStock turns a prescription list into a daily plan. It tells people when a dose is due, keeps a record of what was taken, and warns them before a bottle runs out.

See the feasibility presentation Meet the team

Why it matters

People who take medications regularly struggle to remember doses, track what they have taken, and notice when a supply is running low. The result is missed, delayed, or doubled doses.

  • About one in five new prescriptions in the U.S. is never filled.
  • Roughly half of filled prescriptions are taken incorrectly — wrong time, dose, frequency, or duration.
  • Nonadherence costs an estimated $100–300 billion in U.S. health care spending each year.

Source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report, vol. 66, no. 45, 2017.

What DocStock does

Available on a phone or in a browser, so a patient, a family member, or a caregiver can reach the same plan from any device.

One dashboard

Every medication, dose, and upcoming refill on a single screen.

Dose reminders

Schedules built from the prescription, with a nudge when a dose is due.

Supply tracking

Keeps track of current supply and alerts before a refill runs out.

Interaction screening

Checks a new medication against everything already on the list.

Adherence reports

A record of doses taken and missed that can be exported for a provider.

Caregiver access

Multiple profiles under one account, so one person can manage several.

What DocStock leaves alone

Drawing the line early keeps the scope honest and the product safe.

  • It does not order refills on a user's behalf.
  • It does not give medical advice.
  • It does not log symptoms, mood, or other health metrics.
  • It does not connect directly to pharmacy systems.
