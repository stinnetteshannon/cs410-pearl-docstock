Lab 1 — Product Description

DocStock is a web and mobile app that helps individuals and their caregivers manage their prescriptions by scheduling doses, screening drugs for interactions, and tracking supply, with the goal of increasing medication adherence. DocStock’s number one priority is simplifying what can be a stressful process into something easy.
