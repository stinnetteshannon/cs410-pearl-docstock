Working Draft Feasibility Presentation

Roughly half of filled prescriptions are taken incorrectly, and nonadherence costs the U.S. an estimated $100–300 billion a year. DocStock is a smartphone and web app that turns a user's medications into one schedule with dose reminders, activity logging, refill alerts, and interaction checks. This deck covers the problem, the current versus proposed process flow, what the app will and will not do, how it compares to Medisafe, MyTherapy, and Round Health, the major functional components, and the customer, technical, security, and legal risks with their mitigations.
