Final Feasibility Presentation

Roughly half of filled prescriptions are taken incorrectly, and nonadherence costs the U.S. an estimated $100–300 billion a year. DocStock is a smartphone and web app that turns a user's medications into one schedule with dose reminders, activity logging, refill alerts, and interaction checks. This deck covers the problem, the current versus proposed process flow, what the app will and will not do, how it compares to Medisafe, MyTherapy, and Round Health, the major functional components, and the customer, technical, security, and legal risks with their mitigations.

Working Draft Design Presentation

The design presentation builds on the feasibility work and moves toward the prototype. It adds the core algorithms behind DocStock — dose schedule generation, reminders and missed-dose detection, drug interaction screening, supply tracking and refill prediction, adherence reports, and caregiver access control — along with a draft database schema and a comparison of the real-world product with the prototype. This is a working draft, and the work breakdown structure and GUI mockups are still in progress.
